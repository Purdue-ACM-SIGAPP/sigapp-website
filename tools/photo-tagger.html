SIGAPP Photo Tagger

Local-only tool. Load a team photo, click to place polygon points around a person, close the shape, assign a name, then export. Nothing here leaves your browser.

100%

Scroll the photo panel to pan around while zoomed in.

Click to add points — you'll see the shape fill in live as you go. Double-click (or click back near the first point) to auto-close the polygon. Backspace/⌘Z undoes the last action, Esc cancels the current shape.

Not on the roster? Type a name above and click "Add to list" to add them to the dropdown for reuse — across this photo and any other you load later. Or just leave it typed in and tag as-is; either way works.
